INTERPRETATION
The finite-barrier model is quantitatively useful from about 4-8 nm diameter under this scenario. The largest error occurs at 3 nm, where atomistic, non-parabolic and surface effects are strongest.
The scenario band measures sensitivity to stated input assumptions; it is not a statistically calibrated experimental confidence interval.
All three curves are empirical relationships, not independent raw observations. Their spread measures calibration-choice disagreement.
The lifetime check uses 1/radiativeRate_Hz, not the trap-limited effective lifetime.
